What is QuickNotes?

QuickNotes makes it effortless to jot down ideas, save links, or capture images over any app or screen. You can capture your thoughts instantly and stay in your workflow without having to open a full note file.

How to Use QuickNotes

  1. On iPhone:
    1. Control Center: Swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen, tap the Quick Note icon (a square with a squiggly line), type your note, and tap Save.
    2. Share Sheet: While browsing a website or using an app, tap the Share button, select "Add to Quick Note", and tap Save.
  2. On iPad:
    1. Corner Swipe: Swipe diagonally inward from the bottom-right corner of the screen using your finger or Apple Pencil to pull up a Quick Note instantly.
    2. Keyboard Shortcut: Press the Globe key + Q if you use an external keyboard.
  3. On Mac:
    1. Keyboard Shortcut: Press the Globe/Function key + Q at the same time to open a quick scratchpad.
    2. Hot Corners: Move your mouse cursor to a designated corner of your screen if configured in System Settings.

Features

  • System-Wide Access: Open a quick note window instantly from any screen without interrupting your flow.
  • Rich Content: Add web links, images, tags, and people mentions directly inside your notes.
  • Smart Links & Thumbnails: Highlight text in Safari or link apps to view helpful thumbnail previews when returning to that page.
  • Fast Creation Shortcuts: Access notes easily via Control Center, the Share menu, corner gestures, or keyboard shortcuts.
  • Automatic Syncing: Keep notes updated across iPhone, iPad, and Mac via the Apple Support Guide.
  • Easy Organization: Keep notes in a dedicated Quick Notes folder or move them out to convert them into standard notes.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Globe + Q / Fn + Q Open Quick Note
Cmd + N / Ctrl + N Create New Note
Cmd + K / Ctrl + K Add Link
Cmd + Shift + L / Ctrl + Shift + L Insert Checklist
